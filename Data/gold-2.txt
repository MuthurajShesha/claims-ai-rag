PLAN GOLD-2: POLICY WORDING (SYNTHETIC EXAMPLE)
Clause 3.1 Hospitalization. Inpatient hospitalization of 24 hours or more is covered up to the sum insured.
Clause 3.4 Day-care procedures. A listed set of day-care procedures, including cataract surgery, is covered.
Clause 4.2 Waiting period. Pre-existing conditions are covered only after 24 months of continuous coverage.
Clause 4.5 Joint replacement. Knee and hip replacement is covered after a 24-month waiting period unless caused by an accident.
Clause 5.1 Maternity. Maternity expenses are covered after a waiting period of 36 months, up to 50000 per delivery.
Clause 5.3 Ambulance. Emergency road ambulance charges are covered up to 2000 per hospitalization.
Clause 7.1 Exclusions. Cosmetic surgery, dental treatment (unless caused by accident), and treatment outside India are not covered.
Clause 7.3 Room rent. Room rent is covered up to 1 percent of the sum insured per day.
