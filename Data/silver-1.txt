PLAN SILVER-1: POLICY WORDING (SYNTHETIC EXAMPLE)
Clause 3.1 Hospitalization. Inpatient hospitalization of 24 hours or more is covered up to the sum insured.
Clause 3.4 Day-care procedures. Day-care procedures are covered only for cataract surgery and dialysis.
Clause 4.2 Waiting period. Pre-existing conditions are covered only after 48 months of continuous coverage.
Clause 4.5 Joint replacement. Knee and hip replacement is not covered unless caused by an accident.
Clause 5.1 Maternity. Maternity expenses are not covered under this plan.
Clause 7.1 Exclusions. Cosmetic surgery, dental treatment, and treatment outside India are not covered.
Clause 7.3 Room rent. Room rent is covered up to 0.5 percent of the sum insured per day.
