PLAN PLATINUM-3: POLICY WORDING (SYNTHETIC EXAMPLE)
Clause 3.1 Hospitalization. Inpatient hospitalization is covered up to the sum insured with no room rent limit.
Clause 3.4 Day-care procedures. All day-care procedures are covered.
Clause 4.2 Waiting period. Pre-existing conditions are covered after 12 months of continuous coverage.
Clause 4.5 Joint replacement. Knee and hip replacement is covered after a 12-month waiting period.
Clause 5.1 Maternity. Maternity expenses are covered after a waiting period of 24 months, up to 100000 per delivery.
Clause 6.2 International cover. Emergency treatment outside India is covered up to 20 percent of the sum insured.
Clause 7.1 Exclusions. Cosmetic surgery and non-accidental dental treatment are not covered.
